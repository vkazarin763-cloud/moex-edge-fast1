## Interpretation and limitations

- The regulator workbook reports that the right of unitholders to receive trust-management income is not provided for these 19 funds, and therefore no payment frequency is specified.
- This is about cash distributions from the fund to its unitholders. It does not mean portfolio companies/bonds held by a fund do not pay dividends/coupons into the fund.
- This workbook is a snapshot of management-rule characteristics as of 30.09.2026. It is not a complete historical record of actual cash flows or proof that fund rules never changed.
- Preserve the status as `DISTRIBUTION_RIGHT_NO` for this snapshot, not as proof of a complete historical payment audit.
- Do not use Finam's empty fund dividend endpoint response as evidence of no fund distributions.
- Actual distribution history, where applicable, still requires reconciliation to current management rules and manager disclosure notices.
- No NAV/price adjustment is performed by this report. Deposit comparisons remain `NOT_COMPARABLE` until the overall total-return methodology and all other corporate-action sources are verified.
